Our Philosophy Since 2018

More Than
Just Coffee

Nestled along the scenic waterfront of 301 The Grand Parade in Sans Souci, Ape Cafe was born out of a desire to create a sanctuary where Sydneysiders can pause, breathe in the salt air, and savor meticulously crafted coffee.

We wake up with the dawn at 4:00 AM every single morning so early bird rowers, cyclists, runners, and tradespeople never have to compromise on quality coffee. From our famous Gaytime Signature Shakes to our organic folded omelettes, every dish celebrates honest, vibrant Australian cafe culture.

Ethically Sourced Beans: Carefully selected Arabica micro-lots roasted locally for balanced chocolate, toffee, and berry notes.
Pet-Friendly Gathering: Bring your four-legged best friends! Fresh water bowls and house-made puppuccinos are always ready.
Waterfront Verandah: Take in expansive water views across Botany Bay and the Georges River while you dine.
